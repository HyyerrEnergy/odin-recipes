Spaghetti Recipe

Description

A simple and delicious homemade spaghetti recipe.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Pasta sauce
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Boil water and cook spaghetti noodles.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a skillet.
  3. Add pasta sauce to the beef.
  4. Drain the spaghetti noodles.
  5. Combine everything and serve with cheese.
