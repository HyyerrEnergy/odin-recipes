Homemade Pizza

Description

A simple homemade cheese and pepperoni pizza.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Pizza sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Roll out pizza dough.
  3. Spread pizza sauce over dough.
  4. Add cheese and pepperoni.
  5. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  6. Slice and serve.
