Homemade Tacos

Description

Easy homemade beef tacos for dinner.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Taco seasoning
  • Taco shells
  • Shredded cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Salsa

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet.
  2. Add taco seasoning and water.
  3. Simmer until the beef is fully cooked.
  4. Prepare taco shells.
  5. Fill shells with beef and toppings.
  6. Serve and enjoy.
